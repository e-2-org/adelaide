# Adelaide

## by Simon Faithfull

Adelaide consists of a flow of simple line drawings, the images hinting at an underlying narrative which never quite solidifies. The drawings dissolve in and out of their digital constituents and hang in the limbo of cyberspace like half remembered scenes in the folds of our memory.
